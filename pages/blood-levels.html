Normal Blood Count Ranges

A Complete Blood Count (CBC) is one of the most common blood tests. It measures the levels of different cells in your blood. Here are the typical normal ranges for adults:

Understanding Hemoglobin

Hemoglobin is the protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen. It gives blood its red color. Low hemoglobin levels (anemia) can cause fatigue, pale skin, shortness of breath, and dizziness. Maintaining healthy hemoglobin requires adequate iron, vitamin B12, and folate in your diet.

Normal hemoglobin for men: 13.5-17.5 g/dL. For women: 12.0-15.5 g/dL. Levels below these ranges may indicate anemia.

Blood Sugar Levels

Blood glucose levels are another critical measure of health. Normal fasting blood sugar is 70-99 mg/dL. Levels of 100-125 mg/dL may indicate prediabetes, and 126 mg/dL or above may indicate diabetes.

Cholesterol Levels

Cholesterol is measured through a lipid panel. Total cholesterol should ideally be below 200 mg/dL. High cholesterol increases the risk of heart disease. The balance between HDL ("good") and LDL ("bad") cholesterol is just as important as the total number.

Tips for Healthy Blood

Iron-Rich Diet

Eat lean meats, spinach, beans, and fortified cereals to support red blood cell production.

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water. Blood is about 55% plasma, which is mostly water.

Regular Exercise

Physical activity stimulates blood cell production and improves circulation.

Regular Check-ups

Get your blood tested annually to catch any issues early.
