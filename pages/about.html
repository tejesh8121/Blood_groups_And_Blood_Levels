Our mission, our story, and our commitment to blood awareness

Our Mission

BloodAware is an educational project dedicated to spreading awareness about blood — its types, its components, its importance, and the critical need for donation. We believe that knowledge is the first step toward action, and that everyone should understand the life-saving power of blood.

Our Story

This project was created as a college assignment to demonstrate the power of web technologies — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — in building an informative, accessible, and engaging educational platform. But beyond the technical goal, the project serves a real purpose: to educate people about blood and inspire them to become donors.

BloodAware is built entirely with HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no libraries, no build tools. Just clean, standard web technologies.

Technology

This website is built using only fundamental web technologies:

  • HTML5 — for semantic structure and accessibility
  • CSS3 — for styling, responsive design, and animations
  • Vanilla JavaScript — for interactivity, the compatibility tool, gallery, and form validation

No external libraries, frameworks, or build tools are used. The site works entirely offline once downloaded.
