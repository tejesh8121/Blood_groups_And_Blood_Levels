Who Can Donate?

Most healthy adults can donate blood. Here are the general eligibility requirements:

  • Age: Between 18 and 65 years old
  • Weight: At least 50 kg (110 lbs)
  • Health: In good general health, feeling well on donation day
  • Hemoglobin: At least 12.5 g/dL
  • No recent tattoos or piercings (within 3-6 months)
  • Not pregnant or breastfeeding

Eligibility criteria may vary by country and blood bank. Always check with your local donation center for specific requirements.

The Donation Process

1. Registration

Sign in, show ID, and complete a health questionnaire. Takes about 10 minutes.

2. Mini-Physical

A health check of pulse, blood pressure, temperature, and hemoglobin levels.

3. Donation

The actual blood draw takes 8-10 minutes. About 470ml is collected.

4. Refreshment

Rest for 10-15 minutes with snacks and drinks to replenish energy.

The entire process takes about 45-60 minutes, but the actual blood draw is only 8-10 minutes. You can donate whole blood every 56 days.
